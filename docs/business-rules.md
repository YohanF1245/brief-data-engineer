# Business rules

## Product
A product have a name
A product have an ID reference
A product have price
A product have a stock

## Sales
A sale concerns one product
A sale have a product ID reference
A sale have a date
A sale have a quantity
A sale occurs in 1 shop
A sale have a shop ID

## Shops
A shop have an shop ID
A shop is located in a town
A shop have 0 or N employees
